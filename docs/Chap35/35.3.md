## 35.3-4

> Show that the following weaker form of Theorem 35.4 is trivially true:
>
> $$|\mathcal C| \le |\mathcal C^\*| \max\\{|S|: S \in \mathcal F\\}.$$

Starting from the definition of cover:

$$X = \bigcup_{S \in \mathcal C^*} S$$

By union bound on set sizes:

$$
|X| = \left| \bigcup_{S \in \mathcal C^\*} S \right| 
\le \sum_{S \in \mathcal C^\*} |S|
$$

It follows

$$
\sum_{S \in \mathcal C^\*} |S|
\le \sum_{S \in \mathcal C^\*} \max \\{|S|: S \in \mathcal F\\}
= |\mathcal C^\*| \max \\{|S|: S \in \mathcal F\\}
$$

We have $|\mathcal C| \le |X|$ as from the algorithm GREEDY-SET-COVER, every iteration of the while loop adds a set $S$ with at least 1 new element to $\mathcal C$, so the loop runs at most $|X|$ times.

Combining all the inequalities proves

$$|\mathcal C| \le |\mathcal C^\*| \max\\{|S|: S \in \mathcal F\\}$$
